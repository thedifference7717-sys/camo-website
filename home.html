01 // INTEL BRIEFING

Four things every payment hides

CAMO runs Monero's audited privacy technology, unchanged. Every layer is on for every transaction.

TARGET: SENDER

Ring signatures

Every spend hides among 16 possible senders. Observers can verify it, never trace it.

TARGET: RECEIVER

Stealth addresses

Your address never touches the chain. Each payment lands at a fresh one-time key.

TARGET: AMOUNT

RingCT + Bulletproofs+

Amounts are sealed. The network checks the math balances without seeing a single number.

TARGET: LOCATION

Dandelion++ and Tor

Transactions are relayed through a random path before broadcast, so your IP stays off the record.
